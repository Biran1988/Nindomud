# Bloodline PRAC, training dummy, and job-level rewards

- `prac` shows a **KKGK** section. All jutsu with a `kkg_gate` (including Sharingan/Mangekyo, Byakugan/Tenseigan, and Ice Release) are displayed only there, never duplicated under Ninjutsu or General Skills. Their casting rules and original class teacher requirements are unchanged. Existing saved proficiencies are preserved.
- Mob template **VNUM 6** is the indestructible, non-retaliating practice dummy, even when old builder templates are restored. Spawned vnum 6 mobs ignore global level-based HP/damage scaling, have 1,000,000 full HP, no attack damage, no mob jutsu, and cannot die or award XP/loot/bounties. Their HP is continuously clamped to full at the model layer, including DOT, AoE, summons and direct edits; all normal player hits and jutsu usages still occur for proficiency growth. This is intentionally **not** the attack-blocking Immortal flag. It is a normal targetable mob. A prototype is registered only when absent; placement still uses the existing persistent `spawnpoint add mob 6 <room>` builder command and never overwrites `/data`.
- Staff `levelup <player>` retains ninja level-up behavior. `levelup <player> job <job>` and `levelup <player> <job>` grant one specific job level with XP through `jobs.add_job_xp`, including native incremental permanent bonuses and save for offline players. Existing cap and staff permission apply.
